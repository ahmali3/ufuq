Ufuq Privacy Policy

Effective October 30, 2026

In short: Ufuq collects no personal data. Your location is used only on your phone to work out prayer times and the Qibla direction, and it never leaves your phone. The app has no internet access, no ads, no analytics and no accounts.

Who we are

Ufuq (“the app”) is a free Qibla and prayer times app for Android, made by Ahmed Ali. You can reach us at ahmali.dev@gmail.com.

What the app uses, and why

  • Approximate location (optional). If you allow it, the app reads your approximate location from your phone to find your city and calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. You can instead choose a city by hand, and the app works fully without location permission.
  • Compass and motion sensors. Used only while the Qibla screen is open, to point the compass. Readings are not stored.
  • Notifications and alarms (optional, off by default). If you turn on prayer notifications, the app schedules alarms on your phone so it can show a quiet notification at the prayer times you choose.
  • Your settings. Your chosen city, calculation method, adjustments and notification choices are saved on your phone so the app remembers them.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not have permission to access the internet, so it cannot send your location, settings or any other information anywhere. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal or usage data, and we do not use advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services.

All prayer time and Qibla calculations happen on your phone. The list of cities is built into the app.

Sharing with third parties

We do not share data with anyone, because we do not have any. If you choose to email us from the app, your email app sends that message, and we receive only what you write and your email address. We use it only to reply to you.

Backups

Like most Android apps, Ufuq allows Android’s own backup to save its settings, so they can be restored when you set up a new phone. If you have device backup turned on, your settings are handled by Google under your Google account’s privacy settings, not by us.

Deleting your data

Everything the app stores is on your phone. To delete it, clear the app’s storage in your phone’s settings, or uninstall the app.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. Because the app has no internet access, it will never start collecting data without a new version that you choose to install.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app: ahmali.dev@gmail.com.
